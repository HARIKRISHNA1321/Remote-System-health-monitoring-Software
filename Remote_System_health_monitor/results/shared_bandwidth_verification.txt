SIMULTANEOUS HTB BANDWIDTH VERIFICATION

Configuration:
Normal traffic: UDP port 5201, Queue 0

Normal receiver throughput: 1.71 Mbps
Normal jitter: 13.721 ms
Normal packet loss: 0%

Critical receiver throughput: 8.81 Mbps
Critical jitter: 1.509 ms

Combined receiver throughput: 10.52 Mbps

LIVE HTB COUNTER SAMPLE:
Sampling duration: 30.12 seconds
Normal Queue 0: 1.00 Mbps
Critical Queue 1: 9.00 Mbps
Parent HTB class: 10.00 Mbps

The live sample was collected while both streams were
active, but covered only part of the 60-second test.

CONCLUSION:
The experiment demonstrates differentiated traffic
prioritisation. Critical traffic received substantially
higher throughput and lower jitter than normal traffic.

The shared 10 Mbps parent rate was observed during the
30.12-second HTB sampling interval. The difference
between this sample and the complete iperf averages
should be noted rather than presented as a fully
verified 60-second aggregate rate.
